Severity weight: 3 / 5 (fixed test input)

2% period prevalence × 3/5 severity = 1.2/100.

BCG test example: 2% period prevalence × 3/5 assumed severity = 1.2 index points out of 100. This does not mean a 1.2% chance of illness.

The score combines how common a disease is with its assumed severity: period prevalence (%) × severity weight ÷ 5. Higher prevalence or a higher severity weight gives a higher index. This is a presentation test, not a validated measure of expected harm or personal risk.
